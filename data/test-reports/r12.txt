Colonoscopy performed on 2026-09-15 for patient TEST-0012 by Dr. A. Example after a positive FIT. Preparation was adequate, BBPS 2/2/3 (total 7). The caecum was reached and the terminal ileum was entered; the ileocaecal valve and appendiceal orifice were photographed, and the scope was withdrawn over 10 minutes. A 14 mm pedunculated polyp (Paris Ip) was found in the sigmoid colon and removed in one piece by hot snare after placing a detachable loop at the base; the stalk was clipped and there was no bleeding. A 3 mm polyp in the descending colon was removed by cold forceps. Both specimens were placed in formalin and sent to the pathology laboratory, and the request form gave the FIT result and the sites. The histology has not been reported yet, so the final diagnosis is pending. The patient was advised that the result will be communicated by letter and the follow-up interval will depend on it.
