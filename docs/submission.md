## Video requirements

> Upload your video to YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive or a similar platform, and make sure the link is viewable by anyone who has it. Keep it under 3 minutes, and show what your solution does and how it works.

- Duration **under 3 minutes**.
- The link must open for anyone who has it, without login.
- Content: what the solution does and how it works.

## Conflict of interest declaration

The form asks the team to check everything that applies to any member:

- Employed or contracted by the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi
- Employed or contracted by Future Health or one of the challenge collaborators (Microsoft, Inception42, MBZUAI, or Hack2skill)
- Has a family member or close personal relationship with a judge, organizer, or collaborator staff
- Has a financial interest in the organization running or judging the challenge
- Has had access to non-public information about the challenge or its judging
- None of the above

Every team member must be asked directly before the form is filled in.
